# ppnn Group-Action Audit

This note records a reproducible orbit count for a proposed (^{4}\mathrm{He}=ppnn) Wick-class action. It is separate from the production peram/Dirac reducer: the two count different equivalence relations.

The raw flavor pairing count is (6!6!=518{,}400). Quotienting by the four sink-baryon exchanges of identical-flavor quarks gives (518{,}400/2^4=32{,}400) states. A state consists of two unordered source-(u) pairs assigned to the sink protons, two ordered remaining (u) labels assigned to the sink neutrons, two unordered source-(d) pairs assigned to the sink neutrons, and two ordered remaining (d) labels assigned to the sink protons.

The tested group is generated by the source and sink swaps of the two protons and two neutrons, plus one global (p\leftrightarrow n, u\leftrightarrow d) isospin flip. The four block swaps form (H\cong(C_2)^4); the isospin flip exchanges the proton and neutron generators, so (|H\rtimes C_2|=32).

Burnside fixed-point histogram:

| Fixed states | Group elements |
| ---: | ---: |
| 32,400 | 1 |

| 180 | 4 |
| 96 | 4 |

Thus the action has 1,068 orbits. Direct orbit enumeration gives 4, 14, 83, and 967 orbits of sizes 4, 8, 16, and 32, respectively. The included (ppn) calibration has 360 states and 93 orbits, matching the cited (^{3}\mathrm{He}) number.

The (ppnn) result does not reproduce the 1,107 reported in [arXiv:0912.1383v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0912.1383v1), which describes a computer screening but gives no explicit generator list or survivor list. [arXiv:1205.0585](https://arxiv.org/html/1205.0585) records 32,400 sub-permutations and cites the 1,107 reduction. The discrepancy means this explicit orbit action is not by itself a derivation of the paper's count; it does not establish that the paper's count is incorrect.
