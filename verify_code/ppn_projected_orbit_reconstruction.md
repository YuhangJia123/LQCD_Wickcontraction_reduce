# ppn: 93 Projected State-Block Reconstruction

## Result

The local 93-orbit action does give an exact reconstruction after the correct object is chosen. A representative is not one raw Wick contraction. It is a **state block** formed by summing the 8 signed raw Wicks that share one 360-state label assignment. For the projected pp-spin-singlet channel tested here, every state block in one proton-exchange orbit has the same value. Therefore

\[
F_{\mathrm{pp\,singlet}}=\sum_{s\in S} b_s
  =\sum_{O\in S/G}|O|\,b_{s_O},
\qquad
b_s=\sum_{w:\,s(w)=s}\operatorname{sgn}(w)\,\langle P, C_w\rangle .
\]

There are 360 states, 8 raw Wicks per state, and 93 orbits: 6 orbits have size 2 and 87 have size 4. Thus the 93 representatives carry exact multiplicity weights 2 or 4. The representative's `b_s` already includes its 8 internal Wick terms; 93 does not mean selecting 93 individual raw `contract(...)` calls.

## Why The Weights Are Exact

The projector uses upper Dirac components 0 and 1 and the proton-pair spin singlet with the neutron spin up,

\[
P_{abc}=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}
  (\delta_{a0}\delta_{b1}-\delta_{a1}\delta_{b0})\delta_{c0},
\qquad P_{def}^{*}\text{ at the source}.
\]

It changes sign when the two proton spin axes are exchanged, independently at sink and source. Swapping the two identical proton blocks also permutes a block of three fermion fields, giving a minus sign in the signed Wick block. The two minus signs cancel after projection. Hence a state block and its sink- or source-swapped image have equal projected values. The action generated by these two swaps has the 93 orbits above; summing an orbit is multiplication by its size.

This is a statement about the projected scalar state blocks. Before projection, the corresponding objects are open six-spin-index tensors, and the 93 single-raw-term candidates do not reconstruct their full sum. The local test confirms that distinction: fitted orbit-sum/single-raw-term ratios change with the peram seed, and first-seed ratios predict later full sums with scaled errors 0.197 and 0.113. In contrast, the 8-term state-block ratios agree with orbit sizes to at most `1.2e-14`. Applying the integer orbit weights directly gives same-sample reconstruction errors below `6.7e-17` on all three seeds; weights from the first seed reproduce the other two full sums with scaled errors below `7e-17`.

The script also tests the proton-pair spin-triplet coupled to total spin 1/2 as a control. Its local same-position pp correlator vanishes to floating-point precision, consistent with Pauli antisymmetry, so this channel is not used to establish the 93-term formula.

## Scope

The numerical audit uses the repository's built-in ppn source/sink operators, isospin-degenerate generic complex perams, `Nc=3` epsilon VVV tensors, and three independent seeds. The exact orbit-size identity follows from the explicit block exchange and antisymmetric spin projector described above; the numerical runs check the implementation of that identity.

This identifies a concrete local mechanism by which 93 projected **state-block contractions** reconstruct all 2880 raw Wick contributions. It does not by itself prove that these precise blocks and this projector are exactly the unpublished 93-term implementation in arXiv:0912.1383v1. The paper does not list its survivors, and a fused implementation that evaluates each 8-Wick state block as one computational kernel has not yet been added to production code.
