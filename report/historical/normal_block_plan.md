## Current project convention

- The public selectors are `input`, `mode`, and `output`.
- Supported input types are `distillation` and `normal`; modes are `wick` and `special` (the latter is for `normal`).
- `independent`, `check`, and `block` are Wick-side outputs, while `count` is the normal-special count output.
- The production block path is named `normal-wick-block`; it constructs the raw pairing family automatically. The current acceptance values are ppn = 48 and ppnn = 288.
- Results are written below `result/<input>_<mode>_<output>/`; the checked-in lightweight examples live under `examples/example_result/`.
